Step 1 • 6 to 12 Nov

Problem Statement & The 5-Pillar Breakdown

Class Template: Specific enough that a stranger could go to Lohegaon and verify it.

Official Class Template Formulation:

[Outstation college students moving to Lohegaon/ADYPU] is trying to [find reliable, affordable mess, laundry, and flats/PG within 1.5 km of campus] but [local micro-vendors have zero digital presence, phone numbers on Google are dead, and brokers charge 1-month upfront brokerage], so right now they [walk 5-8 km in the heat asking seniors, click photos of pole posters, or join unmoderated WhatsApp spam groups], which is bad because [they waste 20+ hours, lose ₹3,000–₹8,000 in unfair fees, and get locked into unhygienic 30-day meal passes they quit after 3 days].

1. Complaint

"Google Maps pe number switch off rehta hai. Broker room dikhane ka 1 mahine ka rent maangta hai."

2. Problem

Extreme information asymmetry & zero transparent rate cards within walking distance of college gates.

3. Insight

Students don't need Zomato or Magicbricks. They need a verified 1.5 km campus cheat-sheet with direct WhatsApp contacts.

4. Solution

NearlyY: Hyper-local directory with transparent menus, zero-brokerage badges, and 1-tap WhatsApp leads.

5. Signal

Students and shopkeepers already use makeshift WhatsApp groups to find leads, but suffer from spam and fraud.

Step 2 • 6 to 12 Nov

Map Your Assumptions & Circled Riskiest Guess

10+ hypotheses across Want It, Pay For It, and Can We Deliver It. Tie broken on money.

1

Want It (Demand)

  • Students will use a curated campus directory over physical walking in heat.
  • Transparent rate cards & hygiene ratings matter more than generic star reviews.
  • Students prefer 1-tap WhatsApp chat with shopkeepers over in-app checkout.
  • New admissions & 1st-year hostellers experience the highest daily friction.
★ Circled Riskiest Guess
2

Pay For It (Supply)

  • Local micro-merchants (Mess, Laundry, Gym, PG) will pay an upfront monthly listing fee (₹99 - ₹199/mo) for verified student WhatsApp leads before we have massive traffic.
  • Merchants prefer a flat subscription over 20-30% aggregator commission cuts.
  • Merchants will pay instantly via UPI upon in-person onboarding.
3

Can We Deliver It (Ops)

  • We can onboard 30+ merchants in a 1.5 km radius in under 3 days with field walk-ins.
  • A lightweight mobile PWA / APK provides faster conversion than heavy native apps.
  • WhatsApp pre-filled message templates provide verifiable lead attribution to shopkeepers.
  • Merchant rate cards can be updated seamlessly via cloud sync.
Why this kills the idea if wrong: Students are notorious non-paying customers. If local micro-businesses refuse to pay upfront (demanding 100% free discovery or commission on actual sales), the B2B SaaS model fails instantly. Our survival hinges entirely on merchant willingness to pay.

Step 4 • Checkpoint: Sun 15 Nov

File Your Bet Card (Success & Failure Locked)

Filed before selling. No moving goalposts. Real commitment criteria established.

1. The Offer

  • B2B Merchant Listing: ₹99 Early-Bird Token Advance (Lock-in for 1st Month Listing on NearlyY with verified badge & direct student WhatsApp leads).
  • B2C Student Promise: 100% Free, verified 1.5 km campus radius directory with zero-brokerage flats and student discounts.

2. What Counts as a Commitment

  • ✓ STRICTLY COUNTED: Real money! ₹99 UPI token advance or cash with UTR / payment receipt + signed vendor onboarding sheet.
  • ✕ STRICTLY DISQUALIFIED: Verbal praise ("bahut accha idea hai"), promises to pay later, website clicks without payment, or Instagram likes.

3. Reach Target

  • Pitch 50+ local micro-merchants across Lohegaon, Porwal Road, and ADYPU perimeter face-to-face.
  • Two primary channels: Physical shop walk-ins and direct WhatsApp business outreach.

4. Pass Mark vs Kill Mark

  • PASS MARK: 8 Paying Merchants (₹99 token advance paid upfront).
  • KILL MARK: Fewer than 3 Paying Merchants.
  • MODIFY ZONE: 3 to 7 Paying Merchants (pivot pricing or target category).

Step 7 • 19 Nov • Part 2

The Verdict Call & Strategic Field Learnings

Defense Preparation: "What result would have made you choose differently?"

The Official Verdict Sentence (Done When Format):

"We choose to CONTINUE because 12 out of 15 interviewed students and vendors articulated severe daily discovery friction, and 9 out of 52 pitched merchants committed real money (₹99 UPI advance) before launch.

The evidence that would change our mind is if merchant renewal drops below 40% in Month 2 or if listed merchants receive fewer than 5 student WhatsApp inquiries per week."

1. Mess & PG Urgency

Mess owners experience 30% student churn monthly; PG owners lose ₹10,000/mo on vacant rooms. They converted fastest because lead cost is cheaper than idle capacity.

2. WhatsApp Trumps Dashboards

Shopkeepers refused complicated merchant logins. They loved NearlyY because inquiries land directly inside their personal WhatsApp with zero learning curve.

3. Zero Brokerage = Organic Growth

Students shared the app across campus hostel groups without any marketing spend purely because they found zero-brokerage flat listings with transparent landlord numbers.
